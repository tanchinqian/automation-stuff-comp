### **1.1 Project Overview** 

##### **Real Industry Problem** 

Fluid dispensing is widely used in electronics and semiconductor manufacturing to apply materials such as adhesives, solder paste, epoxy, sealants and other industrial fluids. However, dispensing defects can occur during production. 

Common problems include: 

- Too little material dispensed 

- Too much material dispensed 

- Inconsistent dispensing size 

- Missing dispensing dots 

- Material spreading beyond the required area 

- Air bubbles or abnormal dispensing shapes 

When these problems happen, engineers and operators need to identify: 

- What went wrong? 

- What might have caused the problem? 

- What should they check first? 

Experienced engineers may be able to diagnose these problems quickly. However, new technicians and operators may require more time and experience. This creates an opportunity for AI.

### **1.2 Project Vision Statement** 

Design and develop an AI-powered troubleshooting assistant that can analyse common fluid dispensing problems, identify and rank possible causes, explain its reasoning, and recommend a logical troubleshooting sequence. 

The objective is not to replace engineers. The objective is to create an AI assistant that helps engineers and technicians perform faster preliminary troubleshooting. 

### **2. Project Description** 

##### **2.1 Project Scope** 

How the AI System Works – The basic concept is: 

- User describes or uploads a dispensing problem 

- AI identifies the possible defect 

- AI asks several troubleshooting questions 

- AI analyses possible causes 

- AI recommends what the user should check 

- AI generates a simple troubleshooting report 

### **2.2 Project Completion Criteria** 

The project will be considered as complete when the following criteria are met: 

#### **Step 1 – Dispensing Problem Discovery** 

The AI should first ask approximately five smart questions. 

For example: 

- What material is being dispensed? 

- Is the dispensing amount too large or too small? 

- Is the defect happening continuously or occasionally? 

- Has the material, nozzle or process setting recently changed? 

- Is the defect happening at one location or across multiple locations?
